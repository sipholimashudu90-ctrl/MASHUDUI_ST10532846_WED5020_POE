BRIEF HISTORY

madma Studios is a fictional digital entertainment company established in 2023. It focuses on promoting anime culture by providing fans with a single platform to watch trailers, discover upcoming anime, purchase official merchandise, and read the latest anime news. The company aims to connect anime fans from around the world while supporting creators and studios through legal streaming partnerships.
